# Departure answer guide

Use only fields supported by actual results. This is a guide, not a mandatory
form; a simple question deserves a short answer.

**Recommendation:** route/public name toward headsign, boarding stop/platform,
departure in the agency's local time.

**Basis:** live prediction with observation age, scheduled time, or clearly
stated freshness limitation. Include relevant walking estimate and boarding buffer.

**Alternative:** one later or simpler supported option, if useful.

**Disruption/gap:** a relevant alert or a limitation such as unavailable alerts,
unknown direction, absent walking route, or unconfigured realtime service.

Never supply placeholder values, invented timestamps, or a watch status without
verified evidence. Include public source/map links only when grounded in data.
